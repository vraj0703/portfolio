From slides to pieces

Your three choices, drawn: portrait frames, artwork generated from each project's own facts, and a sheet for the detail. Pick a treatment, then click any piece to walk the interaction.

What changes

Today the frame is a landscape slide: every word about the project is painted on it. The proposal hangs one drawing in a portrait frame and moves the words to a small label beside it.

The wall, in three treatments

Walking past, a visitor sees only the drawing and a name. The two dimmed pieces are the ones still in preparation.

How a visitor moves through it

  1. Walk: drawings and names only. Nothing to read, so the corridor stays a gallery.
  2. Focus: a click or tap brings the camera to one piece. The label beside it is now readable: name, one line, one number, three tags, years.
  3. Sheet: "Read the case" slides a sheet up over the scene with the full story in real, sharp text, and the link out. It closes back to the piece.
  4. Move on: previous and next, by button, arrow key or swipe; Escape steps back one level.

The label's rule
